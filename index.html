📘 Study Notes: Fractions

A fraction represents a part of a whole. For example, 3 4 means three out of four equal parts.

Numerator

The numerator is the top number. It tells us how many parts we have.

Denominator

The denominator is the bottom number. It tells us how many equal parts make the whole.

Example

3 5 Numerator = 3
Denominator = 5

Important Rule

The denominator of a fraction cannot be zero.

Types of Fractions

Type Example Meaning
Proper Fraction 3 5 Numerator is smaller than denominator.
Improper Fraction 7 4 Numerator is equal to or greater than denominator.
Mixed Number 1 3/4 Whole number plus a proper fraction.

Equivalent Fractions

Equivalent fractions have the same value.

1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8

We can create an equivalent fraction by multiplying or dividing the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.

Comparing Fractions

  • For fractions with the same denominator, the larger numerator gives the larger fraction.
  • For fractions with different denominators, we can use equivalent fractions or cross multiplication.
