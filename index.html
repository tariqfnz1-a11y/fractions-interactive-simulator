📘 Fraction Study Notes

A fraction represents part of a whole. For example, 3 4 means 3 equal parts out of 4 equal parts.

🔵 Numerator

The numerator is the top number. It tells us how many parts are being considered.

🟣 Denominator

The denominator is the bottom number. It tells us how many equal parts make the whole.

Example: 5 8
Numerator = 5
Denominator = 8

🌟 Types of Fractions

Type Example Rule
Proper 3/7 Numerator < Denominator
Improper 9/4 Numerator ≥ Denominator
Mixed Number 2 1/4 Whole number + fraction

🔄 Equivalent Fractions

Equivalent fractions have exactly the same value.

1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8

Multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.

⚖️ Comparing Fractions

  • If denominators are the same, compare the numerators.
  • For different denominators, convert them into equivalent fractions or use cross multiplication.

➕ Adding Fractions

When denominators are the same:

2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7

➖ Subtracting Fractions

5/8 − 2/8 = 3/8
